\textbf{BlueDot Impact, Technical AI Safety Project Sprint}\par
Independent research with BlueDot mentorship. Lead researcher on LSR-Anchoring, a training-free activation-steering method that restores safety refusals in low-resource African languages.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Designed the method end to end: prompt-set construction, native-speaker vetting for Igala and Yoruba, evaluation on Llama-3-8B, Llama-3.1-70B, Mistral-7B-Instruct, and Qwen2.5-7B, bootstrap CI analysis, and paper writing.
  \item Recovered near-ceiling refusal on 70B models from English-derived directions (Yoruba and Igbo SRR 0.99); SAE-derived steering removed benign-prompt collapse for Igbo and Igala at 3.5--7$\times$ lower KL divergence than mean-difference steering. First interpretability-based safety work on Igala.
  \item Stress-tested the method against adaptive GCG attacks, replicating Nasr et al. (2025): static anchoring cut English attack success from 1.00 to 0.60, and a white-box adaptive attacker reached only 0.80 at nearly twice the optimizer cost. The English-only anchor fully transferred to Igbo (ASR 0.00).
  \item Identified a language-agnostic refusal feature (Feature 112639, layer 12, EleutherAI SAE on Llama-3-8B); direct logit attribution shows it promotes the Yoruba negation particle \textit{k\`o l\`e} alongside English refusal tokens.
  \item Replicated and extended ACDC-style circuit discovery (Conmy et al., 2023) on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct across English, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igala; circuit-targeted steering preserved benign helpfulness better than global residual-stream steering.
  \item Measured assistant-persona drift over 30-turn conversations on Llama-3-8B in English, Hausa, and Yoruba, finding domain shapes persona more than language, with the weakest anchoring in metacognitive conversations.
  \item Ported cross-lingual red-teaming benchmarks to the UK AI Security Institute's Inspect platform and built \href{https://huggingface.co/spaces/Faruna01/lsr-dashboard}{LSR Workbench}, a public Hugging Face Space for red-teaming and steering experiments.
\end{itemize}
