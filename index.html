I am an AI safety researcher working on multilingual mechanistic interpretability. I am a Research Assistant in the predoctoral program at the Jinesis AI Lab, University of Toronto, with Prof. Zhijing Jin, and a researcher in the AI Explorers program at the University of Michigan with Prof. Rada Mihalcea and Dr. Daryna Dementieva. I am a 2026 grantee of the Cosmos Institute.

My research asks why language models lose their safety behaviour in low-resource languages, and how to restore it without retraining. I study the geometry of refusal across languages, and build training-free interventions such as LSR-Anchoring, which recovers refusals in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and Igala from English-derived directions. I try to break my own results before publishing them, which has meant stress-testing my methods against adaptive attacks.

Before research, I was a software engineer for several years, most recently Senior Software Engineer at Fagmart LLC. I hold an HND in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Kaduna Polytechnic.

Research Interests: mechanistic interpretability (probes, sparse autoencoders, activation patching, circuit discovery), activation steering, cross-lingual safety and robustness, adversarial evaluation, and persona stability in long and multi-agent conversations.
